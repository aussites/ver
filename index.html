Frequently asked questions

Common questions about online casinos in Australia, payments, withdrawals, bonuses, fairness, verification, and responsible gambling.

Under the Interactive Gambling Act 2001, licensed offshore casinos can accept Australian players for most casino games. However, Australian-based operators are not permitted to offer real-money interactive gambling to residents. Most Australians play at internationally licensed sites that operate legally under their own jurisdiction.

Most reputable sites accept Visa, Mastercard, POLi, PayID, and bank transfer in Australian dollars. Some also support e-wallets such as Skrill or Neteller, and a growing number accept cryptocurrency. Always check the cashier before registering to confirm your preferred method is available.

E-wallet and crypto withdrawals are typically processed within 24 hours once identity verification is complete. Bank transfers and card withdrawals can take 2-5 business days. Pending periods — where the casino holds your request before processing — vary from a few hours to 48 hours depending on the site.

Focus on the wagering requirement (how many times you must bet the bonus before withdrawing), the game contribution rates (pokies usually count 100%, table games often less), the time limit to meet the requirement, and any maximum bet restriction while playing with bonus funds. A lower wagering requirement is almost always better than a larger headline bonus amount.

Games from reputable software providers use a certified random number generator (RNG) audited by independent testing labs such as eCOGRA or iTech Labs. Look for casinos that display their RNG certification and published return-to-player (RTP) percentages. RTPs on online pokies typically range from 94% to 97%.

Yes. All regulated casinos require KYC (Know Your Customer) verification before processing withdrawals. You will typically need to submit a government-issued photo ID, proof of address, and sometimes proof of your payment method. Completing this process early avoids delays when you want to cash out.

Most modern online casinos are fully optimised for mobile browsers, so no download is required. Some operators also offer a dedicated iOS or Android app. Game libraries on mobile are generally comparable to desktop, though a small number of older titles may not be available on all devices.

Responsible gambling tools let you control your spending and playing time. Reputable casinos offer deposit limits, session time reminders, loss limits, and self-exclusion options. If you feel your gambling is becoming a problem, you can also register with the National Self-Exclusion Register or contact the Gambling Help Online service at 1800 858 858.
